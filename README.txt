BIRTHDAY WEB APP

1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. The unlock date is 07.09.
3. Replace the letter text inside index.html under <article class="letter-copy">.
4. Replace assets/our-photo-placeholder.svg with your own photo.
   Easiest option: place your image in assets as our-photo.jpg, then replace both
   occurrences of assets/our-photo-placeholder.svg in index.html with assets/our-photo.jpg.
5. The handwritten font loads from Google Fonts. If the phone is offline, it falls back
   to the device's cursive font.

For best mobile testing, use your browser's responsive/mobile mode or open it on a phone.
